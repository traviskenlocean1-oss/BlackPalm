How it works

  1. Send the ideaFill out the form or call. Photos of references help, and you can text them after.
  2. Free consultFifteen minutes with your artist, in the studio or on a call, to talk placement, size and price.
  3. Hold your dateA $100 deposit holds the session and comes off your final price.
  4. Tattoo and healYou leave with an aftercare kit, a day-three check-in and a free touch-up within 60 days.

Before you come in

  1. Eat and hydrateA full meal and water before your session make a long sitting much easier.
  2. Bring ID18 and up with a valid photo ID. No exceptions.
  3. Skip the drinksNo alcohol for 24 hours before. It thins the blood and slows the work.
  4. Wear the right clothesSomething loose that gives easy access to the placement.
