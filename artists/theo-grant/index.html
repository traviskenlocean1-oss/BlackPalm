About the artist

Theo does black and grey realism: portraits, statues, animals and anything that needs smooth gradients and real depth.

Bring a reference photo and he builds the composition around your placement, so the piece reads from across the room.

Booking three to four weeks out. Realism is done in multiple sessions.
