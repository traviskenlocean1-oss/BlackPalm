About the artist

Marco opened Black Palm in 2016 after a decade of tattooing up and down the coast. His work is heavy black, bold flow and pieces that follow the body.

He takes on large-scale back pieces, sleeves and cover-ups, and every project starts with a sit-down consult and a hand-drawn layout.

Booking two to three weeks out. Large pieces are split into sessions of about four hours.
