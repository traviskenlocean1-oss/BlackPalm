About the artist

Dante draws ornamental work the old way, with symmetry, filigree and dotwork that sits clean on the skin for decades.

He loves forearms, hands and chest panels, and he is the one to ask about turning an old piece into something new.

Booking about two weeks out. Walk-in flash on Saturdays.
